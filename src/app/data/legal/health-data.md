Washington’s My Health My Data Act asks businesses to explain any consumer health data they collect. This policy explains how HomunculAi handles it. KrookedLilly LLC publishes HomunculAi.

## What HomunculAi processes and why

HomunculAi checks the messages you write in its chat for phrases about suicide, self-harm, and eating disorders, so that it can show crisis resources. Whether a message matched is the only health-related information HomunculAi creates, and it uses it only to show crisis resources.

## Source

The messages you type in HomunculAi’s chat.

## Where it is kept

Only on your computer, in memory. HomunculAi does not save it, and it is gone when HomunculAi closes.

## Sharing

None. HomunculAi does not send this information to KrookedLilly, to your AI, or to anyone else. Your AI receives the messages you send it, as it does any message; that is between you and your AI provider.

## Third parties and affiliates

None.

## Your rights

HomunculAi stores no health-related information, and KrookedLilly holds no consumer health data about you, so there is nothing to access, share, or delete. You can still write to **support@krookedlilly.com** with any question or request about your health data. We answer within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.
