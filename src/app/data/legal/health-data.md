Washington’s My Health My Data Act asks businesses to explain any consumer health data they collect. This policy explains how HomunculAi handles it. KrookedLilly LLC publishes HomunculAi.

## What HomunculAi processes and why

HomunculAi checks the messages you write in its chat for phrases about suicide, self-harm, and eating disorders, so that it can show crisis resources. Whether a message matched, and a yearly count of the crisis resource notices shown, are the only health-related information HomunculAi creates. It uses them only to show crisis resources and to show you the count.

## Source

The messages you type in HomunculAi’s chat.

## Where it is kept

Only on your computer. Whether a message matched is kept in memory and is gone when HomunculAi closes. The yearly count is kept in HomunculAi’s settings folder until you clear it.

## Sharing

None. HomunculAi does not send this information to KrookedLilly, to your AI, or to anyone else. Your AI receives the messages you send it, as it does any message; that is between you and your AI provider.

## Third parties and affiliates

None.

## Your rights

You can view and clear the count in Settings → About → Crisis resources. Uninstalling HomunculAi and deleting its settings folder removes everything. KrookedLilly holds no consumer health data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, share, or delete. You can still write to **support@krookedlilly.com** with any question or request about your health data. We answer within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.
