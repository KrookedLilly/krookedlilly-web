This notice explains what personal information KrookedLilly receives when you use HomunculAi or contact us about it, and what we do with it. HomunculAi’s Terms of Use (Section 7) describe what the app itself does on your computer.

## Who we are

HomunculAi is made by KrookedLilly LLC (“KrookedLilly”), based in Washington, USA. Contact: **support@krookedlilly.com**.

## The short version

- HomunculAi is free. It has no accounts, license keys, telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting, and it does not send us your activity or settings.
- We receive personal information only when you choose to send it to us: an email, the feedback form, or a tip.
- We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

## What we receive and why

| Information | Where it comes from | Why we use it | Legal basis (EU/UK) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your email address and anything you write to us | You, when you email support@krookedlilly.com | To answer you | Our legitimate interest in answering questions |
| Feedback answers (rating, AI apps used, comments, and, if you choose, your app version, operating system, and email address) | You, through the Google Form that Send feedback opens | To improve HomunculAi and, if you gave an email address, to reply | Your consent (sending feedback is optional) |
| If you tip KrookedLilly: your name or display name, email address, the amount, and any message you add | Ko-fi, when you tip | To thank you and to keep business and tax records | Our legitimate interest; legal obligation (tax records) |

A tip is a voluntary gift to KrookedLilly. It is not a payment for HomunculAi and does not unlock anything.

We do not use your information for automated decisions or profiling, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## What the app sends, and to whom

HomunculAi sends information over the internet only in these cases (Terms of Use, Section 7, has the details):

- **Update checks** (when you select Check in Settings, or each time HomunculAi starts if you turned that on): a request for the list of releases goes to our release page on GitHub. It does not include any usage data.
- **Feedback:** the form opens in your browser on Google Forms. Google receives your answers when the form opens; they are saved as a response only when you select Submit.

Each of these services also receives standard request information, such as your IP address. We do not receive that information from them.

HomunculAi has no chat of its own: you talk to your AI in your AI app, and what you write there goes to your AI’s provider under that provider’s privacy policy. HomunculAi and KrookedLilly never receive it.

## Who else receives information

- **Ko-fi** and its payment providers process tips under their own privacy policies.
- **Our email hosting provider** stores the email you send us.
- **Google** hosts the feedback form (Google’s privacy policy applies).
- **GitHub** hosts our website and release downloads (GitHub’s privacy statement applies).
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

## Where your information is kept

We and our service providers are in the United States, and your information is stored and processed there. If you are in the EU, the UK, or another country with data-transfer rules, our service providers use the transfer safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

## How long we keep it

We keep support email as long as we need it to answer you and to handle any question that follows from it, and then delete it. Tip records stay in Ko-fi and in our business records for as long as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your information earlier where the law allows.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To use any of these rights, write to **support@krookedlilly.com**. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you, for example by replying to the email address you wrote from.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, write to **support@krookedlilly.com** with “Privacy complaint” in the subject line. We confirm that we received your complaint within 30 days (usually much sooner), look into it without undue delay, and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, the authority in your country; in India, the Data Protection Board of India.

## People in India

For people in India, KrookedLilly is the Data Fiduciary for the support, feedback, and tip information described above, and **support@krookedlilly.com** is the contact for questions and grievances about your personal data. You can ask us to access, correct, or erase your information, and to name someone to act for you. We answer grievances within the time the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules allow.

## If something goes wrong

We protect the information we hold with reasonable security measures. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities that the law requires us to tell, without undue delay.

## Children

HomunculAi is only for people aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly receive information about anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.
