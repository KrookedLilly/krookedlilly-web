This notice explains what personal information KrookedLilly receives when you use HomunculAi or contact us about it, and what we do with it. HomunculAi’s Terms of Use (Section 7) describe what the app itself does on your computer.

## Who we are

HomunculAi is made by KrookedLilly LLC (“KrookedLilly”), based in Washington, USA. Contact: **support@krookedlilly.com**.

## The short version

- HomunculAi is free. It has no accounts, license keys, telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting, and it does not send us your activity or settings.
- We receive personal information only when you choose to send it to us: an email or the feedback form.
- We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

## What we receive and why

| Information | Where it comes from | Why we use it | Legal basis (EU/UK) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your email address and anything you write to us | You, when you email support@krookedlilly.com | To answer you | Our legitimate interest in answering questions |
| Feedback answers (rating, AI apps used, what isn’t working, comments, and, if you choose, your app version, operating system, and email address) | You, through the Google Form that Send feedback opens | To improve HomunculAi and, if you gave an email address, to reply | Your consent (sending feedback is optional) |

We do not use your information for automated decisions or profiling, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## What the app sends, and to whom

HomunculAi sends information over the internet only in these cases (Terms of Use, Section 7, has the details):

- **Update checks** (when you select Check in Settings, or each time HomunculAi starts if you turned that on): a request for the list of releases goes to our release page on GitHub. It does not include any usage data. If you choose to download an update, the installer comes from the same place.
- **Feedback:** the form opens in your browser on Google Forms. Google receives your answers when the form opens; they are saved as a response only when you select Submit.

Each of these services also receives standard request information, such as your IP address. We do not receive that information from them.

HomunculAi has no chat of its own: you talk to your AI in your AI app, and what you write there goes to your AI’s provider under that provider’s privacy policy. HomunculAi and KrookedLilly never receive it.

Links in the app open web pages in your browser, such as the KrookedLilly website. Other websites and services that those pages link to have their own privacy policies.

## Who else receives information

- **Our email hosting provider** stores the email you send us.
- **Google** hosts the feedback form (Google’s privacy policy applies).
- **GitHub** hosts our website and release downloads (GitHub’s privacy statement applies).
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

## Where your information is kept

We and our service providers are in the United States, and your information is stored and processed there. If you are in the EU, the UK, or another country with data-transfer rules, our service providers use the transfer safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

## How long we keep it

Email you send us stays in our support mailbox, and feedback answers stay in the feedback form’s responses. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

## Your rights and questions

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, or delete your information, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To use these rights, or to ask a question or make a complaint, write to **support@krookedlilly.com**. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you, for example by replying to the email address you wrote from. You can also contact the data protection authority where you live.

## Children

HomunculAi is only for people aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly receive information about anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.
