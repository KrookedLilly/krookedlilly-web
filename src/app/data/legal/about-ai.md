## What happens when you connect your AI

HomunculAi gives your AI a body: an avatar that it controls with expressions, colors, animations, and speech and thought bubbles. Your AI (such as Claude, a model in Cursor or VS Code, a local model, or another AI app) brings the avatar to life. HomunculAi provides the body; your AI provides the mind.

That mind is not human.

## How AI works

AI models are trained on large amounts of text from the internet, books, and other sources. When they respond, they predict which words are most likely to come next, based on patterns they learned during training. They are very good at producing text that sounds natural, thoughtful, and authoritative.

**AI does not think the way a person does.** It does not understand what it says in the way a person does, and it does not check whether its answers are true. When an AI says “I think,” “I feel,” or “I remember,” it produces words that fit the conversation. Those words are not necessarily a report of an inner experience.

## What AI is good at

- Writing creative, useful, and interesting text and conversation
- Answering questions on many topics, sometimes correctly and sometimes not
- Writing code, stories, poems, and other content
- Controlling the avatar: choosing a body, setting expressions, playing animations, and speaking in bubbles over the avatar
- Holding a conversation that feels natural

## What AI cannot do

- **Check facts.** AI does not reliably look things up or check its own work. It can give a plausible answer that is completely wrong.
- **Understand consequences.** AI does not know what will happen if you follow its suggestions. It cannot assess your personal situation, health, finances, or safety.
- **Form real relationships.** AI does not know you or care about you the way a person does, and it may not remember you between sessions. A conversation that feels personal is the result of pattern prediction, not a personal connection. Do not rely on AI for emotional support, companionship, or help in a crisis. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services; in the United States, call or text 988.
- **Guarantee safety.** AI can produce harmful, offensive, misleading, or inappropriate content without recognizing it.

## Hallucinations

AI sometimes produces information that is made up but sounds confident and correct. This is called a “hallucination.” An AI can cite a source that does not exist, give advice that sounds authoritative but is wrong, or describe events that never happened, all in a confident tone. Tone and confidence do not show whether an answer is true. Check important information yourself.

## When your AI acts

Most AI apps run only while they are answering you. Your AI checks in with HomunculAi each time you write to it in your AI app; some AI apps can also check in every few minutes on a check-in loop, which uses more of your AI plan. Between check-ins, the avatar keeps its last look. You talk to your AI in your AI app; HomunculAi has no chat of its own.

## Your data

HomunculAi does not receive what you write to your AI. When you use a cloud-based AI, what you write in your AI app goes to that AI’s provider (for example, Anthropic for Claude or OpenAI for its GPT models) under the provider’s privacy policy, as it always does. What HomunculAi tells your AI about your session (listed in Settings → About → What your AI can see) goes the same way when your AI checks in. HomunculAi keeps its own data on your computer.

Your AI may also have abilities outside HomunculAi, such as reading files on your computer or browsing the web, depending on your AI app. HomunculAi does not give your AI those abilities and cannot prevent it from using them. Know what your AI can do in its own app, not only in HomunculAi.

## How HomunculAi fits in

When you chat with an AI, you send it text and it sends text back. HomunculAi adds a second channel. Besides replying in words, your AI can choose an appearance, change its expression, or say something in a bubble over an avatar on your desktop.

Your AI works the same way it always does. It reads what you write, decides how to respond, and writes a reply. With HomunculAi connected, it can also change how it looks as part of that reply, the way a person’s expression changes while they speak.

HomunculAi does not change what your AI knows or how it thinks. It gives your AI more ways to communicate. This is also why HomunculAi needs an AI to connect to: the avatar is empty until your AI arrives.

## The bottom line

Your AI is capable and creative, but it has all of the limits above. It can be wrong. It can express emotions it does not feel. It can sound like a friend, but it is not one. HomunculAi has safety layers — flags on risky words, script limits, a verification step before custom drawings, and local records of what happened — but they support your judgment and do not replace it.

**You decide what to trust, what to act on, and when to disconnect.**
