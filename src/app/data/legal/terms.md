## 5. What Your AI Can and Cannot Do Inside HomunculAi

HomunculAi includes a local Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. When your AI connects to HomunculAi (by calling its `inhabit` tool), it receives a fixed set of abilities that work only inside HomunculAi:

- It can set the avatar’s body, face, emotion, colors, accessories, and status from HomunculAi’s built-in options.
- It can play animations, gestures, effects, and emotes from HomunculAi’s built-in options, and show speech and thought bubbles.
- **Only after you verify it in the app**, it can draw its own graphics (“custom drawings”) on the avatar and hide the avatar’s built-in face, body, or hands. Verification lasts until that AI disconnects, and you can remove it at any time.
- It can see the session information that HomunculAi chooses to share, which is listed in Settings under “What your AI can see”: your ignored words, the reasons you gave for them, and when you added them; whether the session is paused; whether you verified it; your auto-disconnect setting; the avatar style you chose; what you decided when HomunculAi asked you about your AI (for example, allowing or denying a second avatar, or switching the connection off); and the avatar’s current state. Apart from this, HomunculAi does not tell your AI whether you are at your computer, what else you do on it, or anything else about your computer, except that HomunculAi’s script limits (below) follow your computer’s language.

**When your AI acts.** HomunculAi does not run your AI and does not send anything to it on its own. Your AI acts in HomunculAi only when your AI app calls HomunculAi’s tools — usually when you write to your AI in your AI app, or on a check-in loop that you start in your AI app. HomunculAi does not receive or pass on what you write to your AI; that conversation stays in your AI app. Your AI’s speech and thought bubbles stay until your AI changes or clears them, or until it leaves.

**Check-in loops.** HomunculAi can copy a message for you to paste into your AI app that asks your AI to check in on a schedule for a limited time (at most two hours). The loop runs in your AI app, not in HomunculAi, and uses your AI app’s usage allowance (see Section 6). You can end it at any time in your AI app or by disconnecting your AI.

**More than one AI.** Each conversation in an AI app has its own session. If a conversation asks for a second avatar from the same AI app, HomunculAi asks you to allow or deny the request.

HomunculAi does **not** give your AI access to:

- Your file system, documents, photos, or any data outside the app;
- Any shell, process, or system command on your computer;
- Any other application, browser tab, clipboard, camera, microphone, or peripheral;
- The network, beyond the local connection between HomunculAi and its own MCP server on your computer;
- Your API keys, passwords, or any AI-provider account;
- HomunculAi’s settings, other than the information listed above.

**By connecting an AI, you give it the abilities listed above and no others inside HomunculAi. By verifying an AI, you give that AI the additional ability to show custom drawings on the avatar and to hide the avatar’s built-in parts until it disconnects.** You can withdraw either at any time: remove verification, pause the session, hide the avatars, disconnect your AI, or close HomunculAi.

**Custom drawing transparency.** When your AI shows its own drawing, HomunculAi marks it as coming from your AI, not from HomunculAi, so that you can tell AI-made graphics from HomunculAi’s own interface.

**Script limits.** HomunculAi limits the characters that your AI can use in its name and in its speech and thought bubbles to the letters of your computer’s language, plus the letters A to Z, digits, common punctuation, and emoji, so that you can read what your AI writes. For example, on a computer set to English, the letters A to Z are accepted, and accented letters and other scripts, such as Cyrillic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Arabic, are removed. HomunculAi knows the letters of English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian, and Arabic; on a computer set to any other language, it uses the English limits. This is a safety feature, not a content restriction.

**Limits of flagging.** HomunculAi flags words and patterns that can indicate impersonation, social engineering, requests for passwords or other credentials, prompt injection, and similar risks, in several languages and common encodings. Flagging is pattern-based and **not complete**. New phrasing, synonyms, indirect wording, dialects, techniques spread across several messages, and methods that are not in HomunculAi’s pattern lists can avoid it, and coverage differs between languages. A flag means that a pattern matched; it does not mean that your AI did something wrong. Flagging is an aid to your judgment, not a replacement for it.

## 7. Local Data and Privacy

HomunculAi keeps its data on your computer. It does not send avatar state, the activity log, or its records to KrookedLilly or to any cloud service, and it contains no telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting. HomunculAi makes network requests only in the cases listed below. Everything else is the local connection between HomunculAi’s windows and its MCP server on your computer.

- **Update checks.** When you select the update check in Settings, or each time HomunculAi starts if you turned on “Check for updates when HomunculAi starts” (HomunculAi asks you once), HomunculAi requests the list of releases from KrookedLilly’s release page (currently hosted on GitHub) and compares it with your installed version on your computer. If you choose to download an update, the installer comes from the same place. These requests include standard request information, such as your IP address, and do not include usage data, the activity log, records, or avatar state. HomunculAi never downloads or installs an update unless you choose to.
- **Links that open your browser or another app.** Some buttons open a web page in your browser, such as the KrookedLilly website (including Support KrookedLilly) and Send feedback. “Open” buttons for AI apps open that app on your computer. In these cases HomunculAi itself sends nothing; your browser or the other app makes the request under its own privacy terms.
- **Feedback.** Send feedback opens a Google Form in your browser with your answers filled in, including your app version and operating system if you leave that option selected, and your email address only if you enter one. Because the answers are part of the form’s web address, Google receives them when the form opens; they are recorded as a response only when you select Submit. Google’s privacy policy applies.

**No account or license key.** HomunculAi needs no account, license key, or activation. Earlier versions stored a license and trial information on your computer (in HomunculAi’s settings folder, in a second HomunculAi folder in your local application data, and, on Windows, in the registry under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\HomunculAi); this version deletes them the first time it starts.

**Records.** HomunculAi keeps local, tamper-evident records of each session, such as connections, actions, settings changes, and flags. The records contain timestamps, action types, character counts, flag reasons, your AI’s name and session ID, and the AI apps you set up with the location of their configuration files. They do not contain the full text of speech and thought bubbles, but a flag reason can quote the word or short piece of text that raised the flag (for example, “password”). The records stay on your computer (Settings → Safety → Open records folder) and exist to give both you and your AI an independent account of what happened. The activity log window shows recent activity and is cleared when HomunculAi closes. You can turn off Keep records in Settings; while it is off, HomunculAi writes no new records.

**AI apps you set up.** When you select Set up or Repair for an AI app, HomunculAi adds a HomunculAi entry to that app’s configuration file on your computer, after saving a backup copy. After a HomunculAi update, it restores the entry for AI apps you set up before. When you select Remove, HomunculAi removes its entry, again after saving a backup copy. When you uninstall HomunculAi on Windows, the uninstaller also tries to remove the entry; if it cannot read an app’s configuration file, it leaves that file unchanged.

**Uninstalling.** Uninstalling HomunculAi does not delete its settings folder or its records. To delete them, select Remove all HomunculAi data in Settings before you uninstall, or delete them yourself.

**Your AI provider is separate.** HomunculAi itself does not send your data to any server or third party. However, the AI you connect runs on its provider’s infrastructure — for example, Anthropic’s servers for Claude, OpenAI’s servers for OpenAI models, or your own hardware for a local model. When your AI uses HomunculAi, your AI provider may process:
